QUIETWINDOWS
============

Quiet Windows down so your games get the whole PC.


IF YOU READ NOTHING ELSE
------------------------

   Double-click:   QuietWindows.bat

   Press the big green button.
   Restart your PC when it tells you to.

   That's it. You're done.


THEN, EVERY TIME YOU PLAY
-------------------------

   Open QuietWindows.bat again and use "Before & after playing".

   Before you play:  press "Quiet Windows down"
   After you play:   press "Wake Windows back up"

   That second one matters. Until you press it, Windows Update is
   switched off on your PC. Press it when you finish playing.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------

   QuietWindows.bat      The menu. This is the one you want.
   Optimize-My-PC.bat    Same fixes, no menu, no questions.
   README.md             What this is and why it works.
   HOW-TO.md             The walkthrough. Written for normal people.
   scripts\              The individual tools the menu runs.
   extras\               Fixes for one specific game only.


THINGS PEOPLE WORRY ABOUT
-------------------------

"Will this break my PC?"
   No. Every change has an Undo button, and it writes down what your
   settings were before it touched anything so it can put them back.

"Do I need to know what any of this means?"
   No. Every button says in plain words what it's about to do.

"Windows keeps asking me for permission."
   That's normal. Anything that changes a Windows setting makes
   Windows ask first. Say Yes.

"I don't want to turn off my antivirus."
   You're not. It keeps protecting your whole PC. It just stops
   re-scanning your game files over and over while you're playing them.
