QUIETWINDOWS
============

Quiet Windows down so your games get the whole PC.


ONE PATH
--------

   1. Double-click:   QuietWindows.bat
   2. Press the big green button:  QUIET MY PC DOWN
   3. Restart when it tells you to.

   That's setup. You only do it once.


EVERY TIME YOU PLAY
-------------------

   Open QuietWindows.bat again -> "BEFORE & AFTER PLAYING"

   Before:  "Quiet Windows down"
   After:   "Wake Windows back up"

   That second button matters. Until you press it, Windows Update
   stays off. Press it when you finish playing.


NO MENU?
--------

   Optimize-My-PC.bat does the same one-time setup with no menu.
